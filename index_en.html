About

I am interested in blocking phenomena, which are characterized by a large scale meridional meandering of the jet stream that persists for about a week. In particular, I am studying the dynamics and predictability of large scale atmospheric circulation variability such as blocking.

I am also studying the dynamical core of an atmospheric general circulation model (AGCM). In particular, I am interested in spectral methods for horizontal discretization.
